Privacy Policy

Jarvis · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant application used exclusively by its owner, Arturo Castillo, with his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, Jarvis accesses the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, Google Drive, Docs and Sheets data through Google APIs.

How data is used

The data is used only to provide features the owner requests: reading and summarizing emails, showing calendar events, finding contacts and files, and drafting emails or events that the owner approves before they are sent or created.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's own computer (macOS Keychain). Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Content may be processed by the AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to generate responses for the owner.

Google API Limited Use

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
